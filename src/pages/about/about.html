Our mission

A direct, honest line between a devotee and a temple.

Daanpay exists because giving to a temple you can't visit in person shouldn't mean handing cash to a stranger and hoping it reaches the right place. We built a simple way to donate directly to verified temples — with a receipt to prove it, every time.

Why we built this

For most devotees who've moved away from their hometown, or simply can't travel, the only option has been sending money through a relative, an agent, or a temple's outdated bank transfer form — with no real confirmation the offering ever arrived.

We started Daanpay to close that gap: one place to find a temple, understand it, and donate directly to it — the same way you'd pay for anything else today, except every rupee is tracked back to the temple it was meant for.

We're not a payment company that added temples. We're built around one purpose — helping an offering reach its destination, transparently.

What we actually do

Four things, done properly, rather than everything, done loosely.

Verify before we list

No temple appears on Daanpay without going through our verification process first — see exactly how, below.

Send it directly

Your donation is routed to the temple's own account — Daanpay is the path, not a holding stop.

Record every rupee

Every donation gets a timestamped receipt the moment it's confirmed — downloadable, and yours to keep.

Protect your payment

Card and UPI details are handled entirely by licensed payment partners — we never see or store them.

How we verify every temple

This is the part that actually earns your trust — so here's exactly how it works.

1

Documentation check

We confirm the temple's registration, trust or committee documentation, and the bank account it operates under, before any listing is created.

2

Identity match

The bank account receiving donations is confirmed to belong to the temple entity itself — never an individual or unrelated third party.

3

Point-of-contact confirmation

We speak directly with a designated temple representative to confirm the listing details, donation purposes, and receipt requirements before going live.

4

Ongoing review

Verified status isn't a one-time badge — listings are periodically reviewed, and any concern raised by a donor is investigated directly with the temple.

Your money and your data, handled properly

We built Daanpay knowing people are trusting us with something personal. That trust is only earned by being boring and careful about security — not by claiming things we can't back up.

If something ever goes wrong with a payment, our support team is reachable directly, and every transaction has a traceable ID from the moment it's initiated.

  • Payments are processed by licensed, RBI-regulated payment gateway partners.
  • Daanpay never stores your card number, CVV, or UPI PIN.
  • All data is encrypted in transit between your device and our servers.
  • Every donation is assigned a transaction ID you can reference with support at any time.
